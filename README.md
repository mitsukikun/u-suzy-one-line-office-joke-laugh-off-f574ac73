It takes the most overused corporate hiring promise — "unlimited growth" — and flips it in the second half: the only thing actually growing in the office is the meeting calendar, while the salary stays exactly where it was. No setup to explain, no tired office cliché, just a sharp twist on a phrase every worker has heard in an interview.
